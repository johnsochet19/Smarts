Services

What we make, and how we make it.

Four core disciplines, each built to order in solid timber. Prices are quoted per commission after a short conversation about the piece.

01

Dining & tables

Dining tables, desks, coffee tables and benches. Solid-slab or traditionally jointed tops, sized exactly to your room and finished to feel good under the hand.

Solid oak, walnut, ash · Live-edge or square · Hand-cut joints · Hardwax-oil finish
02

Fitted cabinetry

Fitted kitchens, wardrobes, media units and built-in storage designed around your space and installed to a millimetre.

Design consultation · Fitted & freestanding · Soft-close hardware · On-site install
03

Architectural joinery

Bespoke internal doors, staircases and architectural timber for renovations and extensions — structural work that's also meant to be seen.

Doors & frames · Staircases · Panelling · Made to architect's drawings
04

Restoration

Careful repair and refinishing of antique and inherited furniture — bringing worn joints, tops and finishes back to life without erasing their history.

Structural repair · Re-veneering · French polish & oil · Assessment first
